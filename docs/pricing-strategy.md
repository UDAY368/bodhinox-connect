# Bodhinox Connect: pricing and pricing strategy (v2, blank-sheet research)

Researched 22 Nov 2026 by a Fable research agent, not anchored on any earlier price. All prices INR, exclusive of 18% GST, unless marked. **Status: draft, awaiting owner lock.**

**In one line:** three self-serve plans at ₹1,499 / ₹3,499 / ₹7,999 per month, 10% off quarterly and 20% off yearly, zero markup on Meta charges, a 14-day trial instead of a free plan, and Enterprise from ₹24,999.

## 0. Confidence notes

- Meta INR rates (₹0.8631 marketing, ₹0.115 utility/authentication/service) come from secondary sources quoting Meta's INR rate card; Meta's doc confirms the rules. Medium confidence.
- Interakt monthly/yearly and Wati monthly/annual prices are from third-party write-ups (Sep–Nov 2026); quarterly figures read from official pages. Medium confidence.
- AiSensy quarterly/yearly and WANotifier yearly per-month figures derived from stated discount %.
- Wati India message rates unknown. FX assumed ₹86/USD, ₹100/EUR.
- No public pricing for GoKwik Engage, LimeChat, Superleap. No survey data on Indian SMB willingness to pay; section 1.3 is inferred.

### 1.3 Indian SMB buying behaviour (inferred)

- Prices shown ex-GST with "+GST" everywhere; GSTIN holders claim input credit.
- Billing is prepaid (subscriptions and message wallets).
- RBI e-mandate: recurring debits up to ₹15,000 need no per-debit authentication — keep monthly charges under that.
- Vendors push quarterly/yearly because monthly churn is high.
- The Oct 2026 service-message charge is fresh: "no markup" is a live selling point.

## 2. Strategy

- **Positioning:** all-in-one at a mid-market price — one tool replacing a WhatsApp CRM, an Instagram DM tool, a store builder and a booking tool. "One bill instead of four."
- **Tiers:** Starter, Growth (highlighted), Scale, plus Enterprise.
- **Value metric:** flat plan fee; plans differ by seats, numbers, AI credits and depth. Every module in every plan; depth is gated. No per-contact pricing; publish honest contact caps.
- **Free plan vs trial:** no free plan; 14-day Growth trial, no card, ₹100 message credit.
- **Discounts:** monthly list, quarterly −10%, yearly −20%; same on seat/number add-ons. Toggle defaults to yearly.
- **GST:** ex-GST with "+18% GST"; show inclusive total before payment; GST invoice with GSTIN.
- **Meta charges:** 0% markup on every plan, prepaid wallet (min ₹500, optional auto-recharge, low-balance alerts); show category and cost per campaign before sending; direct-to-Meta billing on Scale/Enterprise if possible.
- **AI credits:** 1 credit = 1 AI agent reply; assistant actions ¼ credit; writers 1 credit. BYO key on Scale/Enterprise.
- **Enterprise:** from ₹24,999/mo, annual, sales-led, named manager, SLA, invoice billing.
- **Launch offer (till 31 Mar 2027):** 24-month price lock, free assisted setup/migration, double AI credits for 3 months on yearly.
- **Later experiment:** Instagram-only "Lite" at ₹499.

## 6. Pricing FAQ

1. **Are prices inclusive of GST?** No; 18% GST is added. You see the full amount before paying and get a GST invoice with your GSTIN.
2. **Do you mark up WhatsApp messages?** No. You pay Meta's rate from a prepaid wallet.
3. **Are replies free?** Meta gives each number 1,000 free service replies a month; after that ₹0.115 each, including bot/AI replies. Incoming messages are free.
4. **What is an AI credit?** One AI agent reply = 1 credit; summaries/suggestions = ¼. When credits run out, the AI pauses and chats go to your team.
5. **Is there a free plan?** A 14-day free trial of Growth, no card, ₹100 message credit.
6. **Quarterly and yearly discounts?** 10% off quarterly, 20% off yearly, also on extra seats and numbers.
7. **Change or cancel?** Upgrades immediate and pro rata; downgrades/cancellations at period end. Wallet balance stays.
8. **Commission on orders or bookings?** None from Bodhinox.
9. **Can I bring my number from another provider?** Yes; free setup help on yearly Growth and Scale.
10. **Crossing a limit?** Warnings at 80% and 100%; nothing deleted; add capacity or upgrade.

FAQ 7, 9, 10 and the AI pause behaviour describe recommended policies — confirm the product supports them before publishing.

## 7. Rationale and risks

- ₹1,499 matches the market anchor (AiSensy ₹1,500) at half of Wati/Gallabox entry. ₹3,499 sits where most Indian SMBs buy, with flows, AI, Instagram, store and bookings included. ₹7,999 matches WANotifier Premium / DoubleTick Pro at under half of Gallabox Advanced / Wati Business.
- Worked bill (20,000 marketing messages/month): Bodhinox Growth ₹20,761; Interakt Advanced + AI ₹25,779; AiSensy Pro + flows + AI ₹28,850.
- Risks: zero markup gives up a revenue line; low Starter may attract high-support users; AI cost drift; no free plan loses comparison traffic; Meta rate changes (never hard-code rates); competitors re-price often.
- Re-check before publishing: Meta INR rate card; who bills Meta messages; Meta "AI Providers" policy; live Interakt/Wati/AiSensy prices; real AI cost per reply vs ₹0.60–0.80/credit; GST/SAC with a CA; every gate in the matrix is enforceable in the app (knowledge-base, field, product and form caps were assigned by the researcher, not from code).
